Subscribe in Google Calendar

Desktop (required first)

  1. Sign in to the user portal and copy your ICS subscription URL.
  2. Open calendar.google.com on a computer.
  3. Other calendars → + → From URL → paste the HTTPS ICS link → Add calendar.

Android app

  1. After adding on the web, open the Google Calendar app.
  2. Menu → Settings → enable Sync for the new calendar.

Google controls refresh intervals (often hours). Portal sync updates the feed on the server; it does not force Google to re-download immediately.

Prefer desktop “From URL”. The Android app alone is not a reliable place to paste ICS URLs.
